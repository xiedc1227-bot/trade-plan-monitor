## Implemented behavior

- `activation_is_eligible_on` is the shared replay/production policy. It uses the evaluation target's market date; `TRADING_DAY` is exact, `DATE_RANGE` includes both endpoints, and `MANUAL` remains eligible until explicit deactivation.
- Production plan selection, context construction, and every final persistence transaction recheck the same target-date policy. Before-start, after-end, mismatched `TRADING_DAY`, delayed evaluation, and stale rotation cannot commit a new intent, result head, or alert.
- Startup close recovery examines the pending close's historical target date. A target-date-valid pending close can recover after midnight or after range end; an invalid target date cannot create a new action alert.
- Production delivery rechecks active immutable plan identity and current Shanghai market date. An ordinary alert queued while valid but delivered only after expiry is retained and not sent. A cross-day-created `RISK_BREACH` remains deliverable only when its historical target date was valid.
- `GET /alerts` retains historical rows and exposes `currentActionable=false`; such rows have no position prefill. The Vue alert table renders `历史提醒 · 当前不可操作`. The regenerated OpenAPI snapshot SHA-256 is `5dac634614a5bfa5b16c74f14ebd07fe2d350cb1c36d952d0a23a949818d936a`.
