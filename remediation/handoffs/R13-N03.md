## 2. Root cause and repair

The unchanged original responsive test reproduced `documentElement.scrollWidth=412` against `clientWidth=320` at 320px and 200% root text. The first failure prevented its 768/1024/1440 checks and axe scan. The browser trace and source inspection found ordinary layout minima, not a data or trading defect:

- `SymbolDetailRouteView.vue` allowed the history-range flex content and text inputs to keep a large intrinsic minimum at enlarged text.
- The independent-inventory and manual-risk panels used `10rem` and `14rem` grid minima which could exceed their parent at enlarged text; their controls also lacked container-bounded sizing.

The localized CSS now gives the fieldset and labels `min-width: 0`, makes history and related form controls `border-box`, `width: 100%`, `min-width: 0`, `max-width: 100%`, and bounds responsive fact grids with `minmax(min(100%, ...), 1fr)`. Content is neither hidden nor clipped at page level; tables retain their intentional, labelled horizontal scrollers. No font size, test width, axe threshold, or existing responsive assertion was weakened.

The added browser regression leaves the original responsive/axe test intact and verifies at 320px/200% that both full Shanghai timestamp inputs are visible within the viewport, accept their full values, and retain keyboard focus. The route remains the existing composition surface; this is presentational CSS only, so no new component or state layer was introduced.
