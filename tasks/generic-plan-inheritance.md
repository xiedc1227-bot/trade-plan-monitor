# Generic plan inheritance

## GI-01 — Contract

New successor preparations use the exact reviewed predecessor as their baseline. Unmentioned rules and fields retain their values and provenance by product contract; absence never cancels a rule. Current-source semantic directives identify modifications, additions and cancellations by stable rule identity and canonical source citations, without requiring specific wording. Genuine ambiguous targets or instructions remain review questions. Extraction/schema/reference errors are processing failures, not missing business facts.

Historical frozen inputs retain their legacy interpretation contract. No existing review is rewritten. AI candidates cannot approve themselves or reset the continuous account.

## GI-02 — Implementation and acceptance

- Add a versioned predecessor-patch contract and generic source-backed change assessment; keep legacy decoding reproducible.
- Preserve unchanged fields within modified rules and derive no-op retention from equality. Reject nonexistent targets, unsupported changes and contradictory duplicate operations.
- Perform at most two durable automatic correction attempts for classified extraction/validation errors. Preserve cancellation, timeout uncertainty, admission ownership and source/base identity checks. Do not retry unresolved business facts as transport failures.
- Present actual source ambiguity separately from exhausted processing errors; do not ask users to repair internal field names or repeat unmentioned inherited parameters.
- Validate the four actual originals through the ordinary preparation path without per-file correction scripts, plus differently worded retain/modify/add/cancel cases and negative ambiguity/negation cases. Record actual limitations, provider calls and tests.
- Update English and Chinese operations and verification documentation together.
